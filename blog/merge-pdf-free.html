Why Browser-Based PDF Processing Matters

Most online PDF tools ask you to upload your files to their server. This means:

  • Your financial documents, contracts, or personal papers are stored on someone else's server
  • You have no control over how long they keep copies
  • You're at the mercy of their privacy policy (or lack thereof)

Our PDF Tools use pdf-lib to process everything locally in JavaScript. Files are read into memory, processed, and the result is downloaded — the original data never leaves your device.

How to Merge Multiple PDFs

Merging PDFs is the most requested feature. Here's how it works:

  • Drag and drop multiple PDF files into the merger tool
  • Reorder pages by dragging them in the preview
  • Click "Merge" — the combined PDF downloads automatically
  • Free tier handles up to 10 pages; PRO removes all limits

Other PDF Operations Available

  • Split PDF: Extract specific page ranges or split every page into individual files (download as ZIP)
  • Images to PDF: Convert JPG, PNG, or WebP images into a single PDF with adjustable page size
  • PDF to Images: Extract PDF pages as PNG or JPG images with configurable resolution (1x to 2x Retina)
All operations use pdf-lib, jsPDF, and pdf.js — mature open-source libraries that run entirely in your browser. No backend, no upload, no tracking.

Tips for Working with PDFs

  • Check file sizes before merging — large PDFs (>50MB) may take time depending on your device
  • Use ZIP download for split-all operations to get all pages at once
  • For images to PDF, WebP format offers the best compression-to-quality ratio
  • When extracting PDF to images, 2x resolution is ideal for Retina displays
